Our product

Volunteer clubs run on spreadsheets, chat threads and paper. Vault replaces them with one place for the work.

Vault.

A multi-tenant platform where every club gets its own private workspace, with its own members, records and governance.

  • Member directory with dues and RI clearance
  • Treasury with EXCO approvals and audit-ready reports
  • Events, digital attendance and guided project reports
  • An approval inbox with a permanent audit trail
