Our product

One place for the work that keeps a club running.

Volunteer clubs lose hours every week to admin. Vault gives every club its own private workspace for members, money and decisions.

Vault. vaultpro.cloud ↗

Members

A complete directory with dues, RI clearance and lifecycle, visible by role.

Treasury

Income and expenses across accounts, with EXCO approval and audit-ready statements.

Approvals

Proposals, payments and reports flow through one inbox with a permanent audit trail.

Events & attendance

Plan events, check members in digitally and keep records ready for district reporting.

Guided reports

New members can file a correctly formatted project report on day one.

Public club page

Every club gets its own subdomain to showcase projects, without exposing internal data.

Onboarding

A club can apply in about a minute.

We designed joining to be as light as the work it replaces. Three short steps, saved as you type, and a real person reviews every application.

  • Works on any phone, no install
  • Each club fully isolated at the database layer
  • Free for clubs during early access

How we build

Serious software for volunteers.

Clubs trust us with their members and their money. These are the rules we hold ourselves to.

01

Private by design

Every club is a separate tenant. Security rules at the database layer stop any club from reading another's data.

02

Built with the people who use it

Our founder is a Rotaractor. Every feature starts from a real problem a secretary or treasurer told us about.

03

Priced for volunteers

Free during early access. When we charge, it will be a small per-member fee set to cover running costs, so even small clubs can afford it.

04

A modern, tested stack

Typed code, automated builds and a separate test environment, so every change is checked before it reaches clubs.
